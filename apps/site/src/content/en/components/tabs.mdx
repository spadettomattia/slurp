## Accessibility

- WAI-ARIA pattern: `tablist`, `tab`, `tabpanel`; the active tab has `aria-selected`.
- Right and left arrows move the choice, Home and End go to the ends; only the active tab is in the tab sequence.
- The indicator is a graphic extra: the active tab also has stronger text.
