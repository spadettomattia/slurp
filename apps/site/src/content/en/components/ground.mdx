# Ground

The page baseline, to use once at the root: background, typography and focus ring. It renders no element.

## Examples

### At the root

It goes inside `SlurpProvider` (in an app, usually near the root; here the site already has one). It sets `box-sizing: border-box`, removes the body margin, applies Poppins and the theme background (tinted 7% by the accent) and a 2px focus ring.

## Accessibility

- The 2px visible focus is guaranteed on every focusable element: do not remove it.
- Background and text meet AA contrast in light and dark.
