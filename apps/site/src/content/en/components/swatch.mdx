# Swatch

Choosing a colour: one circle per colour, the chosen one with a ring.

<ApiLinks modules={['Swatch']} />

## Group

`SwatchGroup` takes the `options` (`value`, `color` as a hex, `label`) and works like a radio group. A single `Swatch` is for building a group by hand.

<Group />

## Accessibility

- `SwatchGroup` is a `radiogroup` named by `label`; each `Swatch` is a `radio` named by its colour (“Salvia”).
- One Tab stop, the arrows move and select.
- The chosen colour has an `accent-ink` ring, not only the circle's colour. A 44px target.
