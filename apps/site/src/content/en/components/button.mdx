# Button

The primary action: a pill with a `fill`, `soft`, `outline` or `plain` appearance.

<ApiLinks modules={['Button', 'Bubble']} />

## Appearance

`appearance` picks the visual weight: `fill` (solid accent) for the main action of the view, only one; `soft` and `outline` for secondary ones; `plain` for quiet ones.

<Appearances />

## Tone

`tone="neutral"` drops the accent: for actions that should not draw the eye.

<Tones />

## Sizes

`sm`, `md` (default) and `lg`. Even `sm` stays a target of at least 44px.

<Sizes />

## With icons

`icon` goes before the text, `endIcon` after it: they are decorative, the text is the name. For an icon-only button use [Bubble](/api/bubble), which asks for a `label`.

<Icons />

## As a link

With `href` it becomes a real `<a>`: it opens in a new tab and search engines follow it. With a router, pass its link to `as`: `<Button as={Link} to="/profile">`.

<LinkButton />

## Accessibility

- It is a native `<button type="button">` (or an `<a>` with `href`): Enter and Space activate it.
- Focus is a 2px `focus` ring with a 2px offset, on every appearance.
- Icons are `aria-hidden`: the name is the button's text.
