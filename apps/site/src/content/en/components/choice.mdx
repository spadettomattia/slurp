## Accessibility

- The button opens into a `listbox`; options are `option`s with `aria-selected`.
- Arrow down or up open the list on the chosen option; arrows move, Enter or Space choose; Escape closes and gives focus back to the button.
- The chosen option carries a check, not only a colour.
