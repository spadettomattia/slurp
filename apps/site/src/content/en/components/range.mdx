## Accessibility

- It is a native `<input type="range">` with a visible label: arrows, Page up / down, Home and End.
- `formatValue` becomes `aria-valuetext`: you hear “60%”, not “60”.
- The thumb is a 44px target; the filled part is `accent-ink` (3:1 contrast).
