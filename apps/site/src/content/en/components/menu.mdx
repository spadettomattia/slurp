## Examples

### Basic use

`trigger` is the button's content, every `MenuItem` an action; `tone="danger"` for destructive ones, which say so in their text too.

<Basic />

### Choice items

`Menu.CheckboxItem` is an item that is on or off, `Menu.RadioGroup` with its `Menu.RadioItem`s a choice among several. The menu stays open when you change them; the tick always takes the same room, so labels stay aligned.

## Accessibility

- Roles `menu` and `menuitem`; arrows, Home and End to move, Enter to choose.
- Escape closes and gives focus back to the button.
