# Sheet

A modal panel that rises from the bottom edge (full width on phones) or slides in from a side. Same API as Dialog.

## Accessibility

- From the bottom it has a handle at the top: drag it down and the panel follows your finger; past 100px, or with a quick flick, it closes, otherwise it returns. It is only an extra: the Close button and Escape remain.
- It is a native modal `<dialog>`: contained focus, Escape, the rest of the page inert.
- On closing, focus returns to the element that opened it.
- It enters and leaves with a 450 and 200ms movement; with `prefers-reduced-motion`, a 150ms fade.
