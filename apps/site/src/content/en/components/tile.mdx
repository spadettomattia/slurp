### A tile

## Accessibility

- A `<button>` (or a link with `as`): the name is title, subtitle and badge.
- The text uses the theme’s `fg` and `muted`; with very dark or saturated colours check the contrast.
- The blob uses only `transform` and stops with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
