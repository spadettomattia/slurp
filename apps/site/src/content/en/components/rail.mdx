## Examples

### Basic use

`Rail` is the named `nav`, `Rail.Group` a group with an eyebrow, `Rail.Link` a link: with a router, pass its link to `as` (`<Rail.Link as={NavLink} to="/profile">`). Put it in a floating Surface; on phones navigation stays in the Dock.

- Every group is a list named by its title.
