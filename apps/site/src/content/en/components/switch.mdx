# Switch

A toggle for settings that apply at once, without a “Save”.

<ApiLinks modules={['Switch']} />

## Basic use

Like `Checkbox`: the text is the child, `checked` / `onCheckedChange` control it, `name` and `value` go into the form.

<Basic />

## Accessibility

- It is a native checkbox with `role="switch"`: announced as “on” or “off”, Space toggles it.
- On = thumb at the end with a check and a filled track, not only the colour.
- In a form to submit, prefer `Checkbox`.
