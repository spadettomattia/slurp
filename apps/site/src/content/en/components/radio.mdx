## Accessibility

- `RadioGroup` is a `<fieldset>` with `label` as its `<legend>`: the group has a name.
- Native radios: one Tab stop per group, the arrows move and select.
- The chosen radio is a ring with a dot (a shape, not only a colour); in rows the choice also carries a check.
