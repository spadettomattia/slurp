# NumberField

A number field with 44px − and + buttons: it works with the keys, the wheel or the buttons, within the limits you set.

## Examples

### Quantity with limits

`min`, `max` and `step` set the limits and the step; the buttons turn off at the ends. Arrow up and down change the value, Home and End go to the limits.

## Accessibility

- It is a `spinbutton`: screen readers announce value, minimum and maximum.
- The − and + buttons have a name (“Decrease”, “Increase”) and a target of at least 44px.
- A typed value outside the limits is brought back inside when you leave the field or press Enter.
