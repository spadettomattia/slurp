## Accessibility

- The text is a single paragraph: screen readers read it whole, whatever the scroll.
- Both tones (`muted` and `fg`) have AA contrast: the sentence reads before it lights up.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion`, without script or without `IntersectionObserver` it is simply lit.
