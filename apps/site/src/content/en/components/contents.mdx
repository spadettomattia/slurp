### Basic use

Pass the sections with `id`, text and level. Without `active` it follows the scroll by itself (`useScrollSpy`); with `active` you drive it. With fewer than two sections it shows nothing.

## Accessibility

- It is a named `nav`; the section being read has `aria-current="location"` besides the highlight.
- Links go to the section's anchor, so they work without JavaScript too.
