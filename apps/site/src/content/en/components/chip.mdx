# Chip

A compact toggle for filters and options: when selected it is filled with the accent **and** carries a check.

## Accessibility

- It is a `<button>` with `aria-pressed`: screen readers announce “pressed” or “not pressed”.
- State does not rely on colour: a selected chip also carries the check.
- In a filter group give the group a name, for example with `Filters.Group` or `role="group"` and `aria-label`.
