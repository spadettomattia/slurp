## Examples

### One day

`defaultMonth` chooses the month shown. The arrows move by a day or a week, Page up and down change month.

### Disabled days

`isDateDisabled` receives the ISO day and returns `true` to turn it off: here, weekends. A disabled day cannot be chosen and stays in the text.

## Accessibility

- It is a grid (`role="grid"`) with the month in its name; each day is a button with its full name (“Tuesday 8 December 2026”).
- Only one day is in the tab sequence; the arrows move focus without losing the place.
- The chosen day and the days of a period have fill and mark, not colour alone.
