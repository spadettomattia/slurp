## Accessibility

- The button has `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`; the panel is named by `panelLabel`.
- On opening, focus enters the panel; Escape, a click outside or Tabbing out closes it and focus returns to the button.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion` the panel appears with a fade.
