# Toast

Short confirmations at the bottom of the screen, above the Dock: they arrive, get read, and leave.

## Examples

### Basic use

`ToastProvider` at the root, `useToast()` where you need it: `toast({ title, tone, action })`. They stay 5 seconds (`duration`), paused while hovered or focused; at most 3 at once.

## Accessibility

- The list is a `polite` region, present from the start: every new toast is announced.
- The state is an icon plus a word, never colour alone.
- An action ("Undo") stays reachable from the keyboard while the toast is shown.
