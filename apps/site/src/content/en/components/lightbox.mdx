## Examples

### Gallery

`images` lists `src`, `alt` and a caption. `open` and `onOpenChange` control it; `index` the picture shown. Arrows or buttons go to the next and wrap to the first.

## Accessibility

- Native modal: contained focus, Escape, focus returning to what opened it.
- Left and right arrows (reversed in right-to-left languages); the “2 of 3” position is the description.
