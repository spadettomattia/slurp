# Suggest

A text field with suggestions (autocomplete): the field **becomes** the list, growing downward on one surface.

<ApiLinks modules={['Suggest']} />

## Basic use

Type: every word of the text, in any order and regardless of case or accents, is looked for in the options' label and description. Matches are highlighted.

<Basic />

## Controlled

`value` / `onValueChange` for the chosen option (`null` while the text matches none), `inputValue` / `onInputValueChange` for the text. With `openOnFocus` the list opens as soon as the field takes focus.

<Controlled />

## Results from a server

With `filter={false}` Suggest shows `options` as they are: your app filters, for example asking a server at every keystroke.

<Remote />

## Accessibility

- It is a `combobox` with a visible label, tied to its `listbox` with `aria-controls`; the number of suggestions is announced in a `status` region.
- Focus always stays in the field: up / down arrows move the active option (`aria-activedescendant`, disabled options are skipped), Enter chooses, Escape closes and, the second time, clears.
- A press outside or Tab closes the list; the chosen option carries a check, not only a colour.
