# Dock

The floating bar at the bottom that carries all navigation: crumbs, search and actions. It is also the origin of the collapse / expand transition.

## Accessibility

- One boundary: the bar. Crumbs, search and actions are soft pieces inside.
- Actions and panels follow Bloom’s rules: focus enters the panel, Escape closes it and returns to the button.
- On phones the middle crumbs hide and the current one takes the room; with only search it becomes a “Search…” pill.
