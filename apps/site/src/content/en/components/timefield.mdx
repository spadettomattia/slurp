# TimeField

A time of day: the browser’s `time` input, dressed as a Field. Keyboard, touch picker and announcements are the platform’s.

## Examples

### With limits and step

The value is always `HH:MM`. `min` and `max` limit the choice; `step` (in seconds) paces it: 900 goes by quarters of an hour.

## Accessibility

- It is a real `input type="time"`: screen readers announce hours and minutes, and phones open the system picker.
- Always-visible label, hint and error linked with `aria-describedby`, as in every Field.
- The format shown follows the user’s language; the value stays `HH:MM`.
