# DateField

A date field that opens into a calendar: the day (or period) is written in words, in the theme’s language.

## Examples

### One day

The value is an ISO day (`2026-12-08`). The field grows into the calendar and closes when you choose.

<Basic />

### A period

With `range` you pick the start and then the end, in either order; `min` and `max` turn off the days outside the limits.

## Accessibility

- The button states the value (“Arrival 8 December 2026”) and exposes `aria-expanded`.
- Inside is a `Calendar`: a grid of days with full names, arrows, Page up and down, Home and End.
- Focus moves to the current day on opening and back to the field on closing.
