New components: `MultiSuggest` (Suggest with several choices and removable tags), `Timeline`, `AvatarGroup` and `Consent` (a banner where refusing is as easy as accepting). `Table` gains row selection (`selectable`) and a fixed header (`maxHeight`). `Menu` gains `Menu.Group`, `Menu.Separator` and a `shortcut` on each item. `Sheet` can slide in from a side with `side="end"` or `"start"`.
