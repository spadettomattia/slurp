First release of the Slurp design tokens: `--slurp-*` custom properties for the light and dark themes (`tokens.css`, inside `@layer slurp.base`), Poppins `@font-face` rules (`fonts.css`) and typed TS constants (`color`, `hue`, `space`, `radius`, `font`, `vars`, `cssVar()`, `COMPACT_QUERY`). `@slurp-ui/react` re-exports them from `@slurp-ui/react/tokens`, `@slurp-ui/react/tokens.css` and `@slurp-ui/react/styles.css`.
